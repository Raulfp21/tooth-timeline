Part 2 · Superadded vs Successional

SUCCESSIONAL — succeed (replace) a milk tooth. Predecessor existed.

SUPERADDED — super (extra). Erupt behind the row. No predecessor.

Only permanent teeth divide this way. Primary teeth are simply "primary" — never superadded.

1.
Word cue. Successional → succeed → replaces. Superadded → extra → added at back.
2.
Numbers. 5 successional per quadrant (I·I·C·PM·PM) = 20. 3 superadded per quadrant (M·M·M) = 12.
3.
One-line test. Did a milk tooth fall out to make room? Yes → successional. No → superadded.
