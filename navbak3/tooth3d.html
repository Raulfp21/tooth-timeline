CrownVisible above the gum. Covered by enamel. Shape varies by tooth type — chisel (incisor), conical (canine), cusped (premolar/molar).
NeckConstricted junction of crown and root. More constricted in primary teeth, less in permanent.
RootEmbedded in jaw. Covered by cementum. Primary roots splay wider; permanent roots are closer together.
